User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 2026

1. Requesting Data Deletion

Users may request the deletion of personal information associated with their interaction with the Echamila Messenger service.

2. How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your information, please contact Echamila through its official communication channels and clearly state that you are requesting the deletion of your personal data.

The request should include enough information to allow us to identify the relevant interaction or account without unnecessarily providing additional personal information.

3. Processing the Request

After receiving a valid request, we will review it and take appropriate steps to delete personal information that we are required and permitted to delete.

4. Third-Party Platforms

Information processed directly by Meta, Facebook or Messenger may also be subject to their own data policies and deletion procedures.

5. Contact

For data deletion requests, please contact Echamila through its official communication channels.
